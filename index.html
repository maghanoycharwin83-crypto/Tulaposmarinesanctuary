Explore the rich biodiversity of Tulapos Marine Sanctuary — home to colorful reef fish, vibrant corals, and breathtaking underwater scenery in the heart of Siquijor.

Clown Fish

A clownfish is a small, brightly colored marine fish best known for its orange body with white stripes. It lives in warm ocean waters, especially in coral reefs in the Pacific and Indian Oceans.

Damselfish

Damselfish are very important reef fish because they help shape coral reef ecosystems. They are known for being small but extremely bold, fiercely defending their territory against intruders much larger than themselves.

Parrotfish

Tulapos
Explore More

Parrotfish are colorful reef fish found in tropical oceans like the Caribbean and Indo-Pacific. They get their name from their beak-like teeth used to scrape algae off coral and rocks, playing a vital role in reef health.
