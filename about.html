Our Story

A Community's Promise to the Sea

Tulapos Marine Sanctuary is a community-managed marine protected area located in Barangay Tulapos, Enrique Villanueva, Siquijor. Established through the collective effort of local fisherfolk and community leaders, the sanctuary was created to protect and restore the rich marine biodiversity of Siquijor's coastal waters.

The sanctuary spans a significant portion of the coastal reef system of Tulapos, serving as a critical breeding and feeding ground for hundreds of marine species. Its establishment marked a turning point for the local fishing community — transforming the area from an over-exploited fishing ground into a thriving marine reserve.

Today, Tulapos Marine Sanctuary stands as a model of community-based marine conservation in the Philippines, drawing researchers, divers, snorkelers, and eco-tourists from around the world.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Guided by our commitment to marine conservation and sustainable ecotourism, we strive to protect the ocean for future generations.

Our Mission

To protect, conserve, and rehabilitate the marine ecosystem of Tulapos through community-led governance, sustainable fishing practices, environmental education, and responsible ecotourism.

Our Vision

A thriving, biodiverse marine sanctuary where the community of Tulapos lives in harmony with the sea — serving as a beacon of sustainable marine stewardship in the Visayas and across the Philippines.

Community First

We believe that lasting marine conservation begins with empowering the local community. The fisherfolk and residents of Tulapos are the primary stewards and beneficiaries of the sanctuary's success.

Education & Awareness

We actively promote environmental education and marine awareness programs for visitors, students, and the local community to foster a culture of ocean stewardship.

Experiences

What We Offer

Whether you're a seasoned diver or a first-time snorkeler, Tulapos Marine Sanctuary offers unforgettable experiences for everyone.

Snorkeling Tours

Explore the shallow reef areas teeming with colorful fish and vibrant coral formations. Perfect for beginners, families, and non-divers of all ages.

Scuba Diving

Certified divers can explore the deeper reef sections with our licensed local dive guides, discovering the rich biodiversity beneath Tulapos's waters.

Boat Tours

Enjoy a scenic boat ride around the sanctuary's protected waters and neighboring coastal areas, with opportunities for island hopping around Siquijor.

Underwater Photography

Capture the breathtaking marine life of Tulapos with our guided underwater photography sessions — perfect memories to bring home.

Marine Education

Learn about reef ecology, fish identification, and coral conservation through our educational programs for schools and curious visitors.

Coral Planting

Participate in our coral restoration program — plant a coral fragment and contribute directly to the rehabilitation of Tulapos's reef ecosystem.
